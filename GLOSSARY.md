# Cadence

Cadence is a platform where students buy and take online courses.

## Language

### People

**Student**:
A person who buys and takes courses.
_Avoid_: Learner

### Buying and refunds

**Purchase**:
A student paying for a course.
_Avoid_: Order, transaction

**Refund Request**:
A student's request, with their reason, to get their money back on a Purchase. It is approved or declined by the Cadence team.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, cancel my purchase, Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund**:
The money given back to a student when their Refund Request is approved: always the amount they paid on the Purchase.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, cancellation

**Refund Window**:
The 14 calendar days after a Purchase, including the 14th day, during which a student can raise a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, cancellation window
